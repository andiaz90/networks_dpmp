## Summary

Multisector, two-country sticky-price model in which **sectors differ in the degree of price stickiness**. Aggregate PPP deviations are more volatile and persistent than in an otherwise identical one-sector economy: aggregation over heterogeneous sectoral adjustment speeds generates aggregate RER persistence beyond any individual sector's stickiness, helping resolve the PPP puzzle.

## Relevance to NK-IOSOE

- Directly relevant mechanism: our heterogeneous sectoral Rotemberg costs (kappa_i, from Pastén et al.) generate persistent aggregate TCR dynamics through exactly this aggregation channel — a feature to highlight, not apologize for, when discussing slow Q convergence in oil-shock IRFs.
- Strengthens the paper's motivation for sectoral heterogeneity in price stickiness in an open-economy setting.
