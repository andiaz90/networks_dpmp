## Summary

Estimates an SOE-RBC model on a century of Argentine and Mexican data. The pure RBC model (nonstationary productivity shocks) fits poorly; an augmented model with **country-premium shocks and financial frictions** — including an estimated debt-elastic premium — fits emerging-market business cycles remarkably well and assigns a negligible role to trend productivity shocks.

## Relevance to NK-IOSOE

- Precedent for treating the debt-elasticity of the country premium as an **estimated, non-trivial** parameter rather than a vanishingly small calibration — published cover for chii_b = 0.01–0.05 rather than SGU's 0.000742.
- Premium timing: on the debt stock chosen in the current period — same convention as our post-2026-07-08 spec.
