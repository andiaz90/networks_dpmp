## Summary

The canonical statement of the PPP puzzle: how can real exchange rates be so **volatile in the short run** (consistent with financial/monetary shocks under sticky prices) yet revert to PPP so **slowly** — consensus half-lives of **3–5 years** (12–20 quarters)? Shocks that plausibly generate the volatility should die out at the speed of nominal adjustment (1–2 years), not 3–5 years.

## Relevance to NK-IOSOE

- Empirical benchmark for our TCR (Q) dynamics: model half-life ~25–43 quarters at chii_b = 0.05/0.01 is at the persistent end of, but comparable to, the PPP-puzzle consensus range.
- Defends slow Q convergence in oil-shock IRFs as data-consistent rather than a model defect.
