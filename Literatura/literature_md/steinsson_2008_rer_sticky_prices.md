## Summary

Documents that real exchange rates exhibit **hump-shaped dynamics** — a robust fact across nine large developed economies. Standard sticky-price models driven by monetary shocks generate monotonic RER responses and too little persistence. In response to a number of different **real shocks**, a two-country sticky-price model yields hump-shaped RER dynamics, which act as a powerful source of endogenous persistence and allow the model to match the long half-life of the RER.
